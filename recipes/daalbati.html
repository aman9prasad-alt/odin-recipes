Daal Bati Recipe

Daal Bati is a traditional Rajasthani dish that consists of lentils (daal) and hard wheat rolls (bati). It is a hearty and flavorful meal that is often enjoyed during festivals and special occasions.

Daal Ingredients:

  • Toor dal (pigeon peas)
  • Onions
  • Tomatoes
  • Garlic
  • Ginger
  • Spice (garam masala)

Bati Ingredients:

  • Whole wheat flour (atta)
  • Salt
  • Water

Instructions:

  1. Rinse the toor dal and soak it in water for a few hours.
  2. Cook the dal until it is soft and mushy.
  3. Prepare the spice blend and sauté the onions, tomatoes, garlic, and ginger until golden brown.
  4. Add the cooked dal to the sautéed mixture and simmer for 10-15 minutes.
  5. For the bati, mix the whole wheat flour, salt, and water to form a dough.
  6. Divide the dough into small balls and flatten them slightly.
  7. Bake or fry the bati until they are golden brown and crispy on the outside.
  8. Serve the daal with the bati, garnished with ghee, and enjoy!

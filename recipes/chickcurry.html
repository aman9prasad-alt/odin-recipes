Chole Bhature Recipe

This is a delicious and spicy Indian dish.

Chole Ingredients:

  • Chickpeas
  • Onions
  • Tomatoes
  • Garlic
  • Ginger
  • Spice (garam masala)

Bhature Ingredients:

  • All-purpose flour (maida)
  • Semolina (sooji)
  • Yogurt (dahi)
  • Salt
  • Water
  1. soak the chickpeas in water for a few hours
  2. Cook the chickpeas until tender
  3. Prepare the spice blend.
  4. saute the onions until golden brown
  5. Add the cooked chickpeas and spice blend to the onions
  6. Simmer the mixture for 10-15 minutes
  7. For the bhature, mix all the ingredients and knead into a dough
  8. Roll out the dough and fry until golden brown
  9. Serve the chole with the bhature and enjoy!
